20 Action Points (AP) Resource Budget

Detectives start with 20 AP per case. Every interrogation question, autopsy examination, or forensic test consumes 1 AP, requiring efficient deduction before the trail goes cold.

📻

6 Distinct 1980–2010 Era Genres

Filter or procedurally generate cases across Cold War Espionage, Early Internet & BBS, Corporate Raiders, Analog to Digital, Music Industry Bootlegging, and Academic Espionage.

🎭

Dynamic Suspect Emotion Engine

Guilty suspects craft subtle timeline inconsistencies and escalate nervousness when caught. Innocent suspects stay confident in their alibis but guard unrelated private secrets.

📜

Fair-Play Golden Age Mystery Rules

Adheres strictly to Agatha Christie and Byomkesh Bakshi deduction principles: all necessary clues are discoverable through rigorous investigation—never random chance.

Audio Architecture

Zero-Asset Procedural Audio Synthesizer

Real-time PCM sine wave frequency synthesis using low-level Android AudioTrack.

Acoustic Engineering with Zero External Audio Files (SoundManager.kt)

The entire atmospheric soundscape is synthesized mathematically on the fly using Android's low-level `AudioTrack` and 16-bit PCM encoding. APK size remains ultra-compact because zero `.mp3` or `.wav` sound files are packaged.

Sound Event Mathematical Frequency Synthesis Psychoacoustic Function
Typewriter Keystroke 800 Hz short pulse (15ms) with rapid decay Provides tactile haptic & acoustic feedback on typing dialogue dispatches.
Evidence Discovery Ascending sine sweep: 1200 Hz → 1800 Hz Signals critical clue unearthing during autopsy and room searches.
Case Solved Triad Harmonic major triad: 440 Hz → 554 Hz → 659 Hz Rewarding acoustic flourish confirming successful criminal accusation.
Cold Case Buzzer Descending dissonance: 150 Hz → 100 Hz Low-frequency rumble indicating an incorrect accusation or exhausted AP.
