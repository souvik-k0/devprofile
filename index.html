PyaarAI

Download APK Learn More
Romantic AI Companion, Urdu/Hindi Poet & Tactile Scrapbook Studio

PyaarAI is a native Android application engineered to celebrate and deepen modern Indian relationships. Instead of generic conversational wrappers, PyaarAI combines culturally grounded classical Urdu/Hindi Shayari with tactile scrapbook journaling and live physical merchandise simulation. Built on a zero-hallucination prompt engine with anti-duplication fingerprinting in local SQLite Room.

  • AI Poetry & Shayari Studio: 6 dynamic personas (Shayari with strict Qaafiya & Radeef, 2-paragraph letters, 5-R psychological apologies, and physical inscriptions).
  • Physical Gift Mockup Studio: Dynamically renders poetry onto 3D simulated ceramic coffee mugs, heavyweight t-shirts, and acrylic glass frames with dynamic font scaling.
  • Tactile Scrapbook Timeline: 3D interactive Polaroid cards with washi tape accents that flip on tap to reveal private scribbles, paired with procedural torn paper canvas.
  • Anti-Duplication & WhatsApp Sharing: In-memory Compose rasterization shares styled parchment cards directly into WhatsApp with automatic 3-item self-pruning disk cache.

1980–2010 Retro Noir Murder Mystery Simulation & Procedural Case Engine

Redacted is an immersive retro noir detective simulation game set in the gritty analog-to-digital transition era (1980–2010), featuring evocative Kolkata and Bengal backdrops (Salt Lake Cyber Hub, Lalbazar Detective Bureau). The app blends classic Golden Age fair-play deduction (Sherlock Holmes, Byomkesh Bakshi, Agatha Christie) with live generative AI roleplay, wrapped inside a tactile manila case folder aesthetic with zero-asset real-time PCM audio synthesis.

  • 6-Genre Era Matrix: Procedurally generates murder webs across Cold War Espionage, BBS & Early Internet, Corporate Raiders, and Analog-to-Digital transitions.
  • 20 Action Point (AP) Economy: Manage limited AP between interrogations, body autopsies, room searches, and chemical forensics before the trail runs cold.
  • Dynamic AI Interrogation Engine: Suspects lie, deflect suspicion, experience stress surges, and protect secrets. Includes bilingual বাংলা & English.
  • Zero-Asset PCM Audio Engine: Custom software synthesizer using Android's low-level AudioTrack generating real-time sine waves for typewriter clicks (800Hz).

SouvikEQ

Download APK Learn More
Broadcast-Grade System-Wide Audio Equalizer & AI Sound Engineer

SouvikEQ bridges the gap between complex broadcast acoustic engineering tools and everyday consumer listening. Instead of forcing users to guess arbitrary decibels across unfamiliar frequencies, SouvikEQ combines a tactile hardware mixing desk aesthetic with system-wide DSP interception (Spotify, YouTube, Tidal) and an embedded AI Sound Engineer that converts subjective natural language into surgical acoustic curves.

  • System-Wide DSP Interception: Listens to Android broadcasts to master audio from Spotify, YouTube Music, and Apple Music globally.
  • 5-Band Faders & Cubic Bézier Curve: Real-time parametric frequency curve computed dynamically from 20 Hz to 20 kHz with 32-band spectrum analyzer.
  • The AI Sound Engineer (M2M Protocol): Real-time streaming regex parser extracts preset blocks and injects gains directly into hardware DSP.
  • 3D Spatializer & Sub-Bass Exciter: Employs HRTF algorithms to expand headphone soundstage without muddying midrange vocals.

Parchhai

Download APK Learn More
AI Street Chronicle Engine & Living Historical Vault ("Shadows of the Past")

Parchhai resurrects the hidden street history, forgotten colonial police blotters, royal conspiracies, and bizarre underworld syndicates tied directly to the exact soil beneath the user's feet. Built with open-source OSMDroid cartography and a strict Zero-Hallucination Evidence Pipeline (pre-retrieving real Wikipedia geo-archives and verified news before calling the LLM at locked 0.3 temperature), narrated in the theatrical rhythm of 13th-century Dastangoi oral storytelling.

  • Single-Pointer Dynamic Cartography: High-performance dark-mode OpenStreetMap (OSMDroid) with unified dynamic pointer and zero API costs.
  • Zero-Hallucination Pipeline: Enforces minimum 3-source evidence threshold; refuses to fabricate fake history on barren terrain.
  • Spiciness Meter (1 to 10) & Physical Anchors: Categorizes chronicles across 3 epochs with real physical street anchor cues.
  • Dastangoi "Ask AI" Ghost Chat: Interrogate historical witnesses in English or Hindi, backed by regex offline fallbacks.
