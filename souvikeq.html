The AI Sound Engineer: Ending Guesswork

Replacing arbitrary decibel slider guessing with interactive psychoacoustic consultation.

Subjective-to-Objective Acoustic Translation

Everyday music listeners know when their music lacks punch or when vocals sound muffled, but standard graphic equalizers force them to guess numbers like *+4 dB at 230 Hz* or *-2 dB at 3.6 kHz*. SouvikEQ's conversational AI acts as a live mastering engineer, diagnosing gear and genre to inject tuned parameters directly into the DSP hardware.

🎧

Gear & Acoustic Discovery

Inquires about the listener's exact audio gear (e.g., bright IEMs, consumer Bluetooth headphones like Sony XM4, open-back studio cans, or car speakers) and tunes filter curves accordingly.

🎼

Genre-Specific Diagnostics

Pinpoints exact acoustic shortcomings across Hip-Hop, Rock, EDM, Jazz, or Podcasts (muddy low-mids, sibilant 'S' sounds, recessed soundstage) and computes compensatory corrections.

⚡

Machine-to-Machine Protocol

The AI encapsulates curves into machine protocols (`<<<PRESET_DATA {...} PRESET_DATA>>>`), parsed via streaming regex and rendered as an interactive 'Apply AI Preset' button in chat.

🎙️

Hands-Free Voice Input

Integrated `VoiceInputManager.kt` using Android 12+ on-device SpeechRecognizer, allowing listeners to tune sound effortlessly while listening to their tracks.

Frequency Band Acoustic Role Typical Applications
60 Hz (Sub-Bass) Controls fundamental sub-bass frequencies and physical chest rumble. EDM drops, sub-kick resonance, 808 bass punch.
230 Hz (Bass / Warmth) Governs basslines, low-register brass, and vocal body warmth. Scooped to eliminate boxiness; boosted for acoustic intimacy.
910 Hz (Midrange) Shapes acoustic presence, guitar tone, and dialogue intelligibility. Podcast clarity, snare body, vocal presence in crowded mixes.
